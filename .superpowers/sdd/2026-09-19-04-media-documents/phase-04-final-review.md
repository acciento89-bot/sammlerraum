## Retained nonblocking follow-ups

1. **Task1 directory durability:** `packages/storage/src/local-storage.ts:191`, `:203`, `:244`, `:284` do not fsync directory entries after mkdir/rename/unlink. File contents are synced, and publication is atomic for ordinary process-crash recovery; sudden power loss can still lose an acknowledged filename/directory or resurrect a deletion. Permanent tombstones can retry resurrected deletions, but cannot restore a lost original or automatically rebuild an already-READY missing derivative. Retain the approved minor with this precise limit; a stronger power-loss durability promise requires parent-directory fsync and recovery validation.
